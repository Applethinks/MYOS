Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Introduction

MYOS (Make Your Own Site) showcases website design services, examples, and templates. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit this website or contact MYOS.

2. Information collected by the hosting provider

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including visitor IP addresses, for security and service operations. Please review GitHub's Privacy Statement for details about its practices.

3. Information you provide

If you contact MYOS by email or through a linked social account, the information you choose to send may include your name, contact details, project requirements, and message contents. That information is used to respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.

Your email provider or social platform may process information according to its own privacy policy.

4. Cookies and analytics

The website's use of cookies or analytics depends on the features and third-party services enabled on the site. If these services are added, this policy should be updated to explain what information they collect and how it is used.

5. Sharing of information

MYOS does not sell visitors' personal information as part of its stated website-design services. Information may be handled by hosting, email, social media, or other providers when you use their services. Any additional sharing practices should be disclosed here if they are introduced.

6. Data retention and security

Messages sent to MYOS may be retained as needed to respond to enquiries and manage project discussions. The retention periods of third-party services are governed by their own policies. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. External links

This website may link to external websites and services. MYOS does not control their privacy practices. Please review their policies before sharing information with them.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features or information-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact MYOS at myos.webdev@gmail.com.
