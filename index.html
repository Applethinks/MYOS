GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

A few things you might want to know before discussing a project.

How much does a website cost?

We invite you to propose a budget when you contact us. We'll review your requirements and discuss whether the project can be completed within that budget. The final price depends on the agreed scope and complexity.

What kinds of websites can you build?

We're initially focusing on business websites, restaurant websites, and custom projects that fit our current skills and technical capabilities.

Can you build a full online store?

Full e-commerce projects aren't part of our initial service offering. We can discuss simpler business websites that showcase products and provide contact information.

How long will my website take?

The timeline depends on the size of the project, its features, and how quickly the required content and feedback are provided. We'll discuss an estimated timeline before agreeing to start.

Will my website work on phones?

Mobile-friendly design is an important goal for our websites. The final layout and functionality will depend on the agreed project requirements.

How do I contact MYOS?

You can reach us by email or Instagram using the contact links in the section below.
